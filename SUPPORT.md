# Support

LassiNet is **free and open source (MIT)**, self-hosted. Support is community
and **best effort**:

- **Bugs / questions:** open a GitHub Issue or Discussion with version,
  deployment mode, reproduction, and redacted logs.
- **Security:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not open a public issue
  with exploit details or real network data.
- **Feature requests:** describe the real deployment and the problem, not only
  the desired button.

There is **no SLA, no paid support, no warranty** beyond the MIT license and no
telemetry. Never send tokens, `.env`, captures, real logs or customer data.

If you need production/high-availability/compliance guarantees, LassiNet is not
that product; use it as a lab/small-office console alongside OPNsense/Suricata
and evaluate it against your own risk model.
